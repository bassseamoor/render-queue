The loop

  1. Ask for something
    Say it in plain words — “a customer tracker with fields name, email, status”.
  2. It becomes part of your space
    A real object, with its own id, history, and permissions — not a demo.
  3. Close it. It’s still there tomorrow
    Everything persists. Shut the machine, come back — nothing is lost.
  4. Change it later. Nothing is lost
    Add a field, reshape it — your data stays intact. Software evolves, it isn’t replaced.
