# Semantic Evolution Doctrine

**Status:** Architecture doctrine · **Date:** 2026-10-07 · **Owner:** Sebastian Moore

---

## The Correction

The hard problem is not generating semantics. It is maintaining the *right semantics at the right scope as reality changes* — without turning yesterday's useful interpretation into tomorrow's law.

## The Loop

The system already produces the evidence that drives evolution:

```
CURRENT SEMANTIC GENERATOR
        ↓
produces candidate realization
        ↓
build / use / observe
        ↓
Funnel + verification
        ↓
what survived?
what was corrected?
what was explicitly rejected?
what remained intentionally free?
        ↓
update generator's usable semantic material
        ↓
NEXT GENERATION
```

## What Gets Updated

Not this:

> Sebastian chose blue three times → BLUE IS NOW MOOR LAW.

Not even this:

> Sebastian chose blue three times → permanently increase blue probability.

Those three choices may have belonged to one project family. Instead:

```
Context A                    Context B
  known constraints            different constraints
  known design grammar         different grammar
  available generators         overlapping capabilities
  open dimensions              different open dimensions
```

Generators become **contextual and compositional** — not one gigantic evolving "Sebastian model."

**Mechanism:** contexts are defined by their constraint sets. A new request belongs to the context whose constraints it satisfies, and only that context's accumulated corrections apply. Indexed verdicts keyed by context signatures. No neural machinery required.

## No Retraining Needed

The generator evolves because its **inputs and selectable components** evolve:

```
Today:                              Tomorrow:
  generator engine                     same generator engine
+ grammar v4                         + grammar v7
+ current Blueprint                  + new verified recipes
+ current capabilities               + different Blueprint
+ current environment                + additional capabilities
                                     + changed environment
```

Behavior has evolved without retraining anything. When the *generator itself* proves insufficient, revise that machinery too — only then.

**Principle:** Keep stable mechanics stable. Let the semantic resources around them evolve. Replace mechanics only when reality demonstrates that the mechanics themselves are the limitation.

## Freedom Is a First-Class Record

Absence of semantics remains legitimate. Evolution must not progressively specify every variable until no freedom remains.

A mature generator knows *more precisely which dimensions it is free to invent*.

This requires recording deliberate openness with the same care as decisions:

> "This was left open on purpose" — not an absence of records, but a record of absence.

**Verdicts are the training data. Contexts are the scope. Freedom is a first-class record, not an absence of records.**

## The End State

Not maximum semantic density. Instead:

> **Maximum semantic fidelity where meaning exists, maximum generative freedom where it doesn't.**
