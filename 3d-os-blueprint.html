EVERGROVE, the happiness cultivator — 8 poster boards

The concrete v1 build: a small on-demand generator that dreams up happy and money-making situations from flipped needs, and turns them into blueprints that live in a library and grow into real experiences.

The questions & locked answers

Sixteen questions, asked up front, answered from Sebastian's own words — and locked. The builder (Moor) proposes answers; Sebastian disposes. A lock changes only through a new round of questions, never silently.

The 3D OS

Q1 · What is the product?A: A fully 3D operating system for Moor — a 3D environment with 3D tools, one place for everything. LOCKED
Q2 · What do people do in it?A: Be entertained, work, study, create, share — or just observe and consume, or post; any of it. LOCKED
Q3 · What is the mission?A: Help as many people as possible, while building the vision to the fullest extent. LOCKED
Q4 · What are the core UX laws?A: Consistency, easy navigation, and EASE. Ease = accessibility = people actually do it. No hoop-jumping — no open-this-then-that-then-search-then-click chains. LOCKED
Q5 · How do people get around?A: An infinite-scroll-like mechanism — but instead of scrolling through Instagram, they scroll to a 3D place. LOCKED
Q6 · What happens between browsing and playing?A: A smooth transition between browsing and actual gameplay/doing — no hard mode switch. LOCKED
Q7 · Which platforms?A: Very mobile-friendly AND very PC-friendly — both, fully. LOCKED
Q8 · What does the UI look like?A: As minimal UI as possible; when UI appears, very, very clean. Solid color themes or laser UI. More curves, elegance, cohesivity — intentional, custom-built feel, like someone put real thought behind it. LOCKED

The happiness cultivator

Q9 · What does the cultivator generate?A: Procedural hypothetical situations that make someone happy — and hypothetical situations that make them money. LOCKED
Q10 · Whose needs seed it?A: Sebastian's needs — flipped to other people's needs. LOCKED
Q11 · How heavy is the generator?A: Math and easy — a small footprint, generated on demand. It must not take a lot of space. LOCKED
Q12 · What does it feed on?A: Current objectives, needs, and apps — surfacing which existing capabilities to leverage, including capabilities he wouldn't think to leverage, and emitting blueprint specs. LOCKED
Q13 · What is an evergreen here?A: Things that compound joy and value over time — like the YouTube videos that make money for years because they compound — but actual value, not just content. LOCKED
Q14 · How are situations described?A: In great detail — happy places reflecting the Moor product, and the writing must be very good. LOCKED
Q15 · Where do blueprints go?A: Into a library — then a blueprint can be taken from the library and made into an actual experience, connected to all the other experiences people derive value and happiness from. LOCKED
Q16 · What is the platform called?A: Moor — never "More". This page is labeled "Moor-built / made by Moor". LOCKED

The build pipeline

Proof, not promises. v1 builds EVERGROVE — the situation generator, the blueprint emitter, and the blueprint library. The whole 3D OS is blueprinted above and not built in v1.

STEP 3
