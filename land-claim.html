Land claim — how it works

Walk the grid. Unclaimed plots are wilderness — claim one for ◉100. Amber = yours, red = someone else's. Their land blocks you: you can't claim or build on it.

COMMANDS

  • /claim — claim the plot you're standing on
  • /unclaim — release your plot (50% refund)
  • /build — blueprint menu for this plot
  • /balance — your coins
  • /give 500 — debug coins

OPEN DESIGN QUESTIONS

  • Building: blueprints (pick a house, pay, it appears) vs free wall-by-wall building vs describe-it ("a cabin with a porch") and the system prices the resources?
  • Pricing: flat ◉100/plot, or scale with distance from spawn / plot size / neighbors?
  • Upkeep: do claims decay if you never visit? Can claims be sold or traded?
  • Plot size: one fixed grid size, or let players merge adjacent plots?
