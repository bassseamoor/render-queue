The Funnel in Three Stories

Sebastian, 2026-10-07. The same machine, told three ways.

🧱 1. LEGO — for builders 📦 2. Five-Year-Old — for everyone 🏰 3. The Big Table — for kingdoms

🧱 LEGO

You say: "Build me a spaceship." The robot builds a submarine. "Done!"

Problem: you asked for a spaceship.

The Funnel writes your request on paper: "Nobody gets to change this." That's Page 0.

Before building new pieces, look in the giant LEGO box. Use old engine + old navigation + old lights + make new wings = spaceship. Tomorrow: more LEGO than yesterday.

Engine explodes? "WAIT. Keep that." Save what broke, why, how to notice, how to fix. The mistake becomes a tool.

Weird idea? "Try it." Works twice? 🧱 NEW LEGO PIECE.

256 builders all looking. Forward Observer asks "then what?" until it says "I don't know" — then stops guessing and tests.

"Big toolbox doesn't mean the toolbox gets to decide what Sebastian wants."

📦 Five-Year-Old

Put what you said in a box. 📦 "Don't change this one."

"Wait. Do we already have one?" Use it. Have pieces? 🧱🧱🧱 Put them together.

Works? "Yay. Keep it." Broke? "Uh-oh. Why?" Remember so next time: "Oh! I know this one. Don't do that."

Weird idea? "Okay. Show me." Works again? "KEEP THAT."

"Then what?" ... "I don't know." Stop. Try it and look.

Every time we build something, our toy box gets bigger. But the toy box doesn't get to tell you what you wanted. You do.

🏰 The Big Table

Kingdom too hot. King: "Fix everything without making something else worse."

One giant table. Tiny wooden models of everything. One paper in a glass box: "Everybody gets food and water. Kingdom doesn't get hotter."

Every town gets five cups: food, water, tree, house, smoke. Stones every morning. Horses carry them.

Table people play "Then what?" Nobody may say "probably fine" — show the stones or say "I don't know."

Silly idea? "Don't ask if it sounds silly. Ask what happens." Broken things → Bad Box. Good things → Good Box.

Plans fight. Loser gives up good pieces. Bell rings 🔔 when guessing starts: "Stop telling stories. Go try a small thing."

Final check: take the paper out. Did we secretly build the gold castle?

Nobody has to understand the entire kingdom at once.
