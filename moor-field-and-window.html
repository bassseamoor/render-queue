FIELD & WINDOW

How the walkable globe works — without ever building a globe renderer.
One planet. Two views. No globe mesh.

1 · THE PLANET IS A FIELD

Not a mesh. Not chunks. A function.

The whole planet is one continuous function: give it a seed and a spot on the ball, it answers how high the ground is there. Same spot, same answer, forever. No chunks to stitch, no seams, no poles problem — because there's no mesh at all, just math.

2 · TWO VIEWS, ONE PLANET

Far and near are the same field, drawn two ways.

From orbit you see the far view — the sphere you already have, with atmosphere, clouds, water. On the ground you see the window — one sharp patch. Both sample the same field, so they always agree. Dropping in is just a crossfade between them.

3 · THE WINDOW FOLLOWS YOU

Walk around the world. There is no world mesh.

Your position is tracked on the true sphere. The patch is a window that slides along under you, regenerated from the field — identical every time, so nothing ever pops. Walk in a straight line long enough and you come back around. That's walking around the globe, with no globe mesh existing anywhere.

4 · WHY IT'S FAST
