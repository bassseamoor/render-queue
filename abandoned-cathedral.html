Orders are sealed by the quiz funnel (receipt required) — sealing IS the authorization. Sealed packets enter the durable build queue; the dispatcher runs the gate automatically (receipt → authorization → supersession → scope → executor) and the runner executes, verifies, and publishes. Check status reads the queue's public state. This console is the control plane — it never pretends to build.
