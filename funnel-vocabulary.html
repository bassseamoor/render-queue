v1.0336 properties19 categoriesCandidate requirements
NO CLAIM MAY HAVE A HIGHER STATUS THAN ITS STRONGEST INDEPENDENT EVIDENCE.

Key Principles

  • Share information aggressively. Share authority reluctantly.
  • These are candidate requirements, not automatic truth.
  • Each must become testable before entering a Blueprint.
  • Keep destroying unsupported claims until what remains is hard to bullshit.

Anti-Bullshit Questions

For every significant claim, automatically ask:

  1. WHAT EXACTLY IS BEING CLAIMED?
  2. What artifact proves it?
  3. Who produced that artifact?
  4. Is that also the component making the claim?
  5. Can another mechanism verify it?
  6. What would falsify it?
  7. Has that falsification test run?
  8. Does the evidence prove the exact claim, or merely something adjacent?
  9. Is the number measured, calculated, estimated, inferred, declared, or simulated?
  10. Did the work occur, or was an object created saying the work occurred?
  11. Is this functionality implemented, wired, exercised, observed, verified, and independently verified?
  12. Could the same evidence support the opposite conclusion?
  13. What remains unknown?

Optimization Loop

Page 0 → Blueprint → implementation → run → evidence → attack evidence → discover gap → classify gap → smallest justified revision → rerun affected path → independent verification → carry forward what survived → repeat
