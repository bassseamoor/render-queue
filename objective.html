The vocabulary

TUBE

The funnel plus the request, together — the apparatus a request travels through.

CATALYST

The request itself. It drives the reaction; the tube shapes it.

ROD

What comes out of the tube — the extruded, shaped product.

The questions live on the rod, facing outward — so a later tube has something real to grip and cut against. Their answers are open-ended: every tube run fills them fresh, openly. A tube that names the machine before the questions are answered has failed the instrument.
